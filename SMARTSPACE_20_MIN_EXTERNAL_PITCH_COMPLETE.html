Problem Statement: Maharashtra Focus 3 min

Maharashtra is one of India’s largest logistics and warehousing ecosystems. It contains MIDC-linked industrial warehouses, cold storage facilities, pharma-grade units, FMCG godowns, and urban dark stores.

But the discovery and booking process is still highly fragmented. Most seekers rely on brokers, phone calls, and physical visits. Important information such as actual availability, occupancy, facility compliance, and fair price comparison is not transparent.

The major pain points we targeted are:

  • No single, trustworthy platform for warehouse discovery across Maharashtra.
  • Slow manual search and low confidence in listing quality.
  • No intelligent recommendation support for new users and complex requirements.
  • Weak verification flow for ownership and compliance documents.
  • Spreadsheet-style operational management in many owner/admin workflows.
  • Cold-start problem for newly listed warehouses with no interaction history.

SmartSpace solves these through role-based modules, AI-assisted ranking, and secure Supabase-backed operations.

Machine Learning and LLM Intelligence (Main USP) 3 min

Presenter note: mention model architecture and impact, avoid API key details, focus on capability and reliability.

Why Hybrid Intelligence?

  • ML provides deterministic ranking precision across warehouse features.
  • LLM provides natural language understanding and explanation layer.
  • Together, they improve recommendation quality and user confidence.

Five ML Models Used and Why

ModelWhy selectedStrength in SmartSpace
KNNDistance-based nearest profile matchingStrong local similarity ranking for seeker preferences
Random ForestEnsemble tree robustness for tabular featuresHandles mixed warehouse attributes reliably
Gradient BoostingSequential residual improvementCaptures nuanced non-linear scoring patterns
Neural NetworkLearns latent cross-feature interactionsSupports non-obvious recommendation relationships
Hybrid StackingMeta-combination of base model outputsFinal balanced score with better stability

Presentation-Worthy Performance Lines

  • Recommendation accuracy around 87.9% in your reported benchmark context.
  • Conversational reliability reported around 96.8% in chatbot/assistant flow testing.
  • Precision@10 around 0.83 indicates high top-result relevance quality.
  • Hybrid architecture mitigates single-model weaknesses and improves production trust.
