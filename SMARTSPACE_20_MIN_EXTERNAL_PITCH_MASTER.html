Section 2: Problem Statement (Maharashtra Context) 3 min

Maharashtra has one of India's largest warehousing and logistics ecosystems, including MIDC zones, cold storage infrastructure, pharma-grade facilities, FMCG hubs, and dark stores used by quick-commerce companies.

However, discovery and booking are still fragmented and mostly manual. Businesses often depend on brokers, phone calls, and physical visits. There is no single trusted platform where they can compare verified options with real-time occupancy and intelligent recommendations.

Core pain points we identified:

  • No centralized warehouse visibility
  • Manual and slow decision cycle
  • Weak trust and verification at listing level
  • No intelligent matching for user intent
  • Cold-start issue for new listings in traditional recommendation systems
  • Spreadsheet-driven operations causing data inconsistency

So our problem statement was clear: build a trusted, role-based, AI-assisted warehouse platform specifically aligned to Maharashtra logistics realities.

Section 3: Solution Positioning - What SmartSpace Is 2 min

SmartSpace is a full-stack warehouse intelligence and operations platform with three role-based modules: Seeker, Owner, and Admin.

Our platform combines:

  • Machine Learning ensemble for ranking and matching
  • LLM layer for conversational assistance, semantic parsing, and recommendation explanation
  • Supabase Postgres with Row-Level Security for secure multi-role workflows
  • End-to-end booking lifecycle with activity logs, status transitions, and analytics impact

Stack summary: React + TypeScript + Vite frontend, Express backend, Supabase data layer, shared AI/ML services.

Section 8: ML Focus - 5 Model Explanation and Why 2 min

Our recommendation engine uses 5 models to balance precision, stability, and ranking quality:

  1. KNN: nearest-pattern match for proximity-based relevance
  2. Random Forest: robust handling of mixed features
  3. Gradient Boosting: iterative improvement for difficult cases
  4. Neural pattern layer: captures non-linear behavior
  5. Hybrid stacking: combines strengths of multiple estimators into final rank

Why this set? Because no single model is enough for this domain. Ensemble strategy improves generalization and stability across diverse warehouse profiles.

Presentation metric line: Our measured recommendation performance is around 87.9% with strong top-result relevance, making recommendations practical and reliable for real decisions.

Shared ML files: shared/advanced-ml-algorithms.ts, shared/ml-algorithms.ts, shared/recommendation.ts.

Section 9: Complete Booking Workflow (USP Story) 1.5 min

Booking lifecycle is one of our strongest USPs because it is fully traceable and role-connected.

  1. Owner creates listing submission.
  2. Admin reviews and approves listing.
  3. Seeker discovers listing, selects blocks, submits booking.
  4. Owner approves or rejects booking request.
  5. System updates booking status and occupancy impact.
  6. Activity logs and dashboards reflect real state transitions.

Critical consistency point: booking transitions and activity logs are aligned to avoid analytics drift.
